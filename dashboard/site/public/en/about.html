About the AI Labor Market Index

The AI Labor Market Index tracks, month by month, how artificial intelligence is affecting the Norwegian labor market, built on register data for every private-sector employee.

Method and sources · Cite the index · Research paper: Hernæs and Kostøl (2026), RFBerlin Discussion Paper 179/26 (PDF) · Contact: andreas.r.kostol@bi.no
