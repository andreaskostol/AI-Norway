Seasonally adjusted private-sector employment, index 100 in November 2022. Occupations are split into five equal groups by AI exposure (Eloundou et al. 2024). The figures show the most and the least exposed fifth. The index numbers use a three-month moving average, as on the index page, which also has the uncertainty, more measures and the method. Source: the A-ordningen via microdata.no.

The index compares employment in the occupations where language models can do most of the work with the occupations where they can do least. The numbers are register data for the whole Norwegian private sector and are updated every month. A positive number means the most exposed occupations have grown the most since the event, a negative number that they have grown the least.
