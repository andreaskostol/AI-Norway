Studio

Under construction. The first content arrives during autumn 2026.

Studio will hold three things: podcasts, videos and news on new research on AI and work, including new findings from Anthropic.

  • Podcast. Conversations about one paper or one finding at a time.
  • Video. Short explainers using the figures from this site.
  • News. Notes on new research, written as the papers arrive.
