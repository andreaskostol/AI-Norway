How much is AI used in Norway, and what do we know about tokens and users?

Short answer: only Anthropic publishes country numbers that can be followed over time. OpenAI gives no figures for Norway beyond single statements in press releases. OpenRouter shows only the ten largest countries. This page collects what exists, with Norway against nine countries we like to compare ourselves with. The numbers cover conversations in Claude.ai, not the number of users and not tokens.

Summary for the impatient reader
  • Norway uses Claude a lot per capita. The per capita index is around four, on a par with Switzerland and Iceland and well above Germany.
  • Norwegian conversations are more augmentative than American ones. About 40 percent of classified conversations are automation, against about 50 in the US.
  • Nobody publishes user counts per country. Anthropic gives shares of conversations. Tokens exist only globally, for the API.

1 · Use per capita

Anthropic's own index: the country's share of all Claude.ai conversations divided by its share of the world's working-age population. One means use in step with population.

2 · Automation over time

The share of conversations where the person delegates the task, against the share where the person and the model work together. The definitions are the same as on the occupations page.

3 · Work, personal or coursework

What the conversations are about. Coursework is strikingly high in Norway and rose from April to May 2026. That may be the exam season.

4 · Norway in numbers

Every point in time Anthropic has published with Norway as its own row. The first three are one week each from the raw releases. The last two are monthly files from June 2026, where only the two aggregate buckets are given.

Share of global use: percent of all Claude.ai conversations in the sample. Per capita index: published by Anthropic only in the monthly files. Interaction types are shares of classified conversations.

Tokens and users at the large providers

Anthropic. The Economic Index is the only source with real microdata aggregated to countries. It covers Claude.ai, that is chat and Cowork. API use, where developers and agents call the model, is published only globally. Token and cost figures exist in the API files, but not by country. User counts are not published.

OpenAI. No downloadable country figures. What exists about Norway is a press release on Stargate Norway, where the number of weekly active ChatGPT users in Norway is said to have quadrupled in a year. The level is not given. OpenAI's own usage studies cover the US or the world as a whole.

OpenRouter. The State of AI 2025 report rests on 100 trillion tokens and gives real token counts, but only for the ten largest countries. Norway sits in a residual group of 60 countries. Geography is billing country, not place of use.

Other sources. Third-party traffic estimates put Norway high in Europe in the share who have tried generative AI. They rest on surveys and web traffic, not platform data, and cannot be combined with the numbers above.

Method

The source is the Anthropic Economic Index on Hugging Face. The three weekly samples are taken from the raw data files in the September 2025, January 2026 and March 2026 releases. The monthly numbers come from the country file in the June 2026 release. Automation is directive plus feedback loop, augmentation is task iteration, validation and learning. Unclassified conversations are left out of the denominator. The per capita index is Anthropic's own. Our own calculation with UN population figures lands 25 to 30 percent lower, so it is not shown.

A new release is expected every quarter. The page is updated when Anthropic publishes a new country file.
