\begin{frame}{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
  \item A public monthly dashboard, \textbf{kiindeksen.no}: employment in the universe of Norwegian private-sector payroll jobs, tracked by AI exposure with roughly a three-month data lag.
  \item Test whether the ``canary in the coal mine'' pattern (Brynjolfsson et al.) generalizes: selected high-exposure occupations vs.\ the \textbf{full exposure distribution}.
  \item Study the recent impact of \textbf{Agentic AI} (\emph{``autonomous LLM-based systems that plan, use tools, and execute multi-step research tasks''}, Korinek 2025).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
