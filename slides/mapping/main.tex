\begin{frame}{Roadmap}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
  \item \textbf{Eloundou et al.\ (2024):} task-level LLM exposure rated by humans and GPT-4
  \item \textbf{Handa et al.\ (2025):} revealed usage from Claude.ai conversations
  \item \textbf{Felten et al.\ (2021):} AI progress on the abilities required by each occupation
  \item \textbf{Anthropic (2026):} time-weighted observed Claude usage at occupation level
\end{itemize}
\vspace{1em}
Common destination: a 4-digit STYRK-08 score for every Norwegian occupation.
\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Why a mapping step is needed}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{8pt}
  \item All four measures are built on US occupation codes (SOC).
  \item Norwegian register data use STYRK-08.
  \item STYRK-08 $\equiv$ ISCO-08 at 4 digits (SSB Notater 17/2011).
  \item Bridge: SOC $\to$ ISCO-08 via BLS crosswalk (Aug 2012, rev.\ June 2015).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\section*{Comparison}

\begin{frame}{Four measures, one destination}
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{lllc}
\toprule
Measure & Input unit & US code system & STYRK coverage \\
\midrule
Eloundou (2024)   & O*NET task ($E_1$, $E_2$)              & SOC 2018 & 97.5\,\% \\
Handa (2025)      & Claude conversation $\to$ O*NET task   & SOC 2010 & 86.5\,\% \\
Felten (2021)     & O*NET ability $\times$ AI progress     & SOC 2010 & 95.8\,\% \\
Anthropic (2026)  & Time-weighted Claude usage             & SOC 2010 & 95.3\,\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{1em}

\justifying\small
All four measures reach STYRK-08 via the BLS SOC$\to$ISCO crosswalk. They differ in the input unit (task vs.\ ability vs.\ conversation), in whether exposure is theoretical or revealed, and in whether automation and augmentation are separated.
\end{frame}
