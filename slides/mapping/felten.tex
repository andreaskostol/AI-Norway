% Felten et al. (2021): AI Occupational Exposure (AIOE)

\begin{frame}{What Felten et al.\ measure}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
  \item Ten AI application benchmarks track progress over time:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item image recognition, language modeling, speech recognition, reading comprehension, translation, abstract strategy games, real-time video games, visual Q\&A, image generation, instrumental track recognition
  \end{itemize}
  \item Each O*NET ability is rated for relevance to every AI application
  \item AIOE for an occupation $=$ sum over abilities of (ability prevalence + importance) $\times$ AI progress on that ability
  \item Language Modeling sub-index restricts to the LM benchmark only
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

Two inputs (AI progress, ability importance) combine at the SOC level. The downstream chain is identical to the other measures.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Worked example: Pharmacists (SOC 29-1051)}
\centering\scriptsize
AI progress score for selected O*NET abilities (from Appendix D):

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
O*NET ability & Language Modeling & Reading Compr.\ & Image Recognition & Avg.\ across 10 apps \\
\midrule
Deductive Reasoning      & 0.80 & 0.87 & 0.65 & 0.73 \\
Inductive Reasoning      & 0.79 & 0.84 & 0.69 & 0.73 \\
Written Comprehension    & 0.83 & 0.91 & 0.57 & 0.64 \\
Oral Comprehension       & 0.91 & 0.69 & 0.37 & 0.61 \\
Number Facility          & 0.50 & 0.64 & 0.67 & 0.63 \\
Information Ordering     & 0.85 & 0.92 & 0.87 & 0.88 \\
Memorization             & 0.86 & 0.93 & 0.83 & 0.84 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5em}
\justifying\small
AIOE weights these AI-progress scores by each ability's prevalence and importance in the occupation (from O*NET). For Pharmacists, the weighted sum gives:

\vspace{0.4em}
\centering
\textbf{AIOE $= 0.606$ (Q4)} \quad LM sub-index $= 0.571$ \quad Image-Gen sub-index $= 0.478$
\end{frame}
